Staff performance — rolling window

Each staff is graded against the per-event goals — not against teammates. ≥95% On Track · 65–94% Inconsistent · <65% Underperforming · <3 events Limited Data. Turnout isn't counted (staff don't control who walks in).
How the score is calculated

Step 1 · Score each KPI. Take the staff's average for that KPI and compare it to the per-event goal: (avg ÷ goal) × 100%. Capped at 150%, so going way over goal helps but doesn't snowball. Example: a staff averaging Mail = 6 on EEC events scores 6 ÷ 4 = 150%.

Step 2 · Group into two tiers.

  • Core goals (counts double) — App ≥ 3, TLL ≥ 1, Mail ≥ 4.
  • Bonus goals (counts single) — Interplay ≥ 1, CARE/FERA ≥ 1, MBL ≥ 1, ESA ≥ 1. Newer to track, so missing them docks the staff less and hitting them helps less.

Step 3 · Combine. Final score = (core average × 2 + bonus average) ÷ 3. A staff who's strong on core but weak on bonus won't get hammered.

Step 4 · Only relevant KPIs count. TLL / Mail / Interplay → EEC events (Lionel). App → CO + IQP events (Ada + Veronica). CARE/FERA + ESA → events tagged "Income-Qualified Programs (IQP)". MBL → events tagged "Medical Baseline (MBL)".
